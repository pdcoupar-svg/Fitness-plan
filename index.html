★ The 5 Things to Know

  • 3Not skipping meals is as important as what you eat — especially for your patterns around stress and overwhelm.
  • 4The emergency bag and 9pm supper are your two most important habits — everything else builds on these.
  • 5Consistency over perfection — 3 weights sessions a week done imperfectly beats a perfect plan abandoned.

Restock weekly. Treat like charging your phone.

Snacks and emergency backup

ItemProteinNotes

Water bottle—Non-negotiable
Protein powder sachet25gAdd to water or milk anywhere
Electrolyte tablet or sachet—One per day in water bottle
Why electrolytes matter for you: Mounjaro causes electrolyte loss, creatine increases water needs, and you don't reliably feel thirst. Low electrolytes cause fatigue, headaches and muscle cramps. Daily electrolyte tablet (LMNT, Nuun, or supermarket own-brand). Look for sodium, potassium and magnesium.
The rule: Some days you won't want the lunch you brought, or there won't be anything suitable nearby. The substantial items above mean you can always make a real meal from your bag.

2. The 9pm Supper Rule

Going to bed hungry = Kit Kats and crisps at midnight. Eat something at 9pm. Every night.

  • Greek yogurt + nuts or honey
  • Cottage cheese + anything
  • Rye bread + nut butter
  • Small bowl of leftovers

🍱 Work Lunches

Eating out at £10 a time is unsustainable. Batch cooking largely doesn't work in practice.

Ammi's Kitchen Sheffield — local kitchen run by women from different cultures cooking authentic family recipes. All vegetarian, naturally high fibre, rotating menu (Punjabi, Bengali, Sri Lankan, Iraqi and more). £16–18 BYO or £20 delivery. One order = 3–4 portions = lunches for most of the week.

Protein reality: These meals are carb and fibre rich but moderate protein. Always supplement with cottage cheese, hard boiled eggs, or a Squeaky Bean pack. This combination hits your protein targets and costs well under £10 a day.

🏋 Training — 5 Days a Week

The Week

DaysActivity
3 daysWeights
2 daysRun or swim (your choice each week)
2 daysRest

Swap days freely. Goal is 3 weights + 2 cardio across the week — not a rigid schedule.

Weights — What You Already Know

  • Chest press
  • Lat pulldown
  • Deadlift
  • Squats
  • Bicep curl ← always in every session
  • Tricep extension ← always in every session
  • Most machines

Do 3 sets of 10 reps on each. When all 3 sets feel comfortable, add a small amount of weight next session.

The One New Exercise to Add First

Seated Cable Row — works your back, essential for posture and upper body balance.

▶️ Watch: How to do the Seated Cable Row (2 min tutorial)

Sitting down, pulling a handle toward your stomach on a cable machine. Ask gym staff to point you to it first time. Once comfortable (2–3 weeks), we'll add the next one.

If very sore from weights, swim instead of run — much easier on the body.

→ The Mounjaro Transition (Autumn)

By the time you come off, these should feel automatic not effortful:

  • Eating 3 times a day
  • High protein meals that feel normal
  • Exercise you look forward to
  • Emergency bag as second nature
  • 9pm supper preventing evening binges
More muscle now = higher resting metabolism = more forgiving when Mounjaro appetite suppression is gone.
